# Oracle Discord Bot Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** October 15, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to Oracle! This Privacy Policy explains how Oracle ("the Bot") handles information when you use it on Discord. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is used. ## 2. Information We May Collect Depending on the features you use, Oracle may process the following information: - **Discord User IDs:** To identify users and provide personalized bot features. - **Discord Server IDs:** To identify servers where Oracle is installed and keep server-specific settings separate. - **Command Inputs:** Information you submit when interacting with the Bot, such as commands or requests. - **Saved Preferences and Settings:** Information needed to remember your preferences or maintain features between sessions. Oracle does not intentionally request sensitive personal information. Please avoid submitting private or sensitive information through bot commands. ## 3. How Information Is Used Information processed by Oracle may be used to: - Respond to commands and requests. - Provide astrology-themed or entertainment features. - Save preferences and server settings when supported. - Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Bot. - Protect the Bot against misuse or technical issues. ## 4. Data Storage and Retention Information may be processed temporarily or stored when necessary for Oracle to function. The type of information retained and the length of storage depend on the Bot's implementation and features. Information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable legal requirements. ## 5. Sharing of Information Oracle does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and any third-party services required to operate the Bot. Such services may handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. ## 6. Third-Party Services Oracle operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies and terms. If Oracle uses external APIs or hosting providers, information needed to provide the Bot's features may be processed by those providers. ## 7. Data Security Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by Oracle. However, no system can guarantee absolute security. ## 8. Your Choices and Requests You can stop interacting with Oracle at any time. If Oracle is installed in a server you manage, you can remove it from that server. For questions about information associated with you, or to request deletion of stored information where applicable, contact the developer through the Bot's official support channel. ## 9. Children's Privacy Oracle is intended for use in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law. The Bot is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. ## 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as Oracle develops. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. ## 11. Contact For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the developer through Oracle's official support channel. By using Oracle, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this Privacy Policy.
